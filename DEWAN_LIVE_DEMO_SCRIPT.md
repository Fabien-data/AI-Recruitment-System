# Dewan Consultants — LIVE DEMO SCRIPT
**Read this aloud while showing the system on screen.**
**Each section = one menu item. Same order as the left sidebar.**

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

For every section, you'll see four blocks:

1. **🗣 SAY THIS** — the words to speak as you click into the page.
2. **👀 POINT AT** — what to physically point to on screen.
3. **✋ DO THIS** — the click/action to perform live.
4. **➡ NEXT STEP** — what to say to flow naturally into the next section.

---

## DEMO SETUP (do before the workshop)

- Log in as **Admin** (full sidebar visible)
- Open in **full-screen browser** (F11)
- Have a real candidate ready to point at — pick one with a complete CV, photo, and a conversation history
- Have one **job** open in another browser tab (already created — for "Knowledge Base sync" demo)
- Optional: have your phone with WhatsApp open, scanning the Dewan bot QR — so you can show a *live* message arriving in front of the audience

# PRESENTER'S DO / DON'T

**DO:**
- Pause after each section, ask "any questions on this one?"
- Use the real Sinhala/Tamil phrases when showing the bot — it gets a reaction
- Walk around the room during the "Story" part (Part C) — it's the climax
- Let the audience touch the dashboard themselves at the end (5 min hands-on)

**DON'T:**
- Don't use words like *API*, *webhook*, *backend*, *server*, *database* — replace with "the system"
- Don't apologise for empty data — if a section has no records, say *"once we go live tomorrow this will fill up"*
- Don't read screen labels word-for-word — explain WHY each thing matters
- Don't rush the Story (Part C) — it's the most important part
