# Dewan Consultants — AI Recruitment System Launch
**Presenter's walkthrough script + complete use-case scenario**
*(Workshop tone. Plain language. No tech jargon.)*

## 0. Opening Line (30 seconds)

> "Good morning, team. Today we're launching a new way of working at Dewan. Until now, every CV, every WhatsApp message, every follow-up call has been handled by a person, on paper or in a chat. From today, we have a digital assistant working alongside you — 24 hours a day, in three languages, never tired, never forgetting a candidate. Let me show you what it does."

## 1. THE BIG PICTURE — What Are We Looking At?

Think of the system as **two halves working together like a left hand and a right hand**:

### ✋ The Left Hand — The WhatsApp Chatbot
This is the **face the candidate sees**.
It's a smart WhatsApp number that talks to job seekers in **English, Sinhala, or Tamil** (even Singlish and Tanglish — the mixed forms our people actually use). It greets them, asks about the job they want, collects their CV, and answers their questions — all on its own.

### ✋ The Right Hand — The Dashboard (RecruitPro)
This is **what you (the Dewan team) see** on your computer screen.
It's a clean website where you see every candidate, every job, every conversation, every interview — all in one place. No more scattered files, lost WhatsApp messages, or forgotten follow-ups.

### How they work together
When a candidate finishes chatting with the WhatsApp bot, **their full profile appears automatically in your dashboard** — name, age, country preference, experience, CV attached. You didn't lift a finger. You just open RecruitPro and the candidate is waiting for you to review.

> **Workshop pause:** *"How many of you today spend more than 2 hours just chasing CVs on WhatsApp? Keep that in mind — we'll come back to it."*

---

## 2. MEET THE COMPONENTS — A Guided Tour

I'll walk you through each section of the dashboard the way you'll use it daily. We start from the left menu and go top to bottom.

### 2.1 Overview (the home page)
The **first screen** every morning. Like the dashboard of a car:
- How many new candidates came in today, this week, this month
- How many jobs are open
- How many interviews are scheduled
- Charts showing where candidates are coming from, which projects are hot
- A "needs attention" section — any candidate the chatbot couldn't handle, any conversation stuck

**Why it matters:** You walk in, glance once, and know exactly what your day looks like. No need to ask "what's pending?" — it's all there.

---

### 2.2 Candidates
A **single list of every person** who has ever contacted Dewan through the bot, through email, or who you added manually.
- Search by name, phone, skill, country preference
- Filter by status (new, shortlisted, interviewed, hired, rejected)
- Click any candidate to see their full story: CV, photo, every WhatsApp message they sent, every job they applied to

**Why it matters:** Right now, finding "that Tamil-speaking driver candidate from last month" means scrolling through WhatsApp. Here, you type *"driver Tamil"* and you have him in 2 seconds.

---

### 2.3 Jobs
This is **where you create and manage every job opening** Dewan is recruiting for.
- Name of the job (e.g., "Heavy Vehicle Driver — Saudi Arabia")
- Country, salary range, age limit, height requirement, languages needed, benefits
- How many people are needed (vacancies)
- Deadline to close the job

**The magic part:** The moment you create or edit a job here, **the WhatsApp chatbot learns about it instantly**. If a candidate messages "Saudi driver job ekak thiyanawada?" — the bot now knows the answer because you just typed it in.

---

### 2.4 Projects
A **project** is the bigger umbrella — for example *"Al-Rashid Construction — Riyadh Site"* — and under one project you can have **several jobs** (drivers, masons, electricians, cleaners).

**Why it matters:** Instead of treating every role as a stand-alone vacancy, you can see *"Out of 50 workers Al-Rashid needs, we've filled 18 so far. Drivers are done, we still need 12 masons."*

---

### 2.5 Applications
Every time a candidate applies for a specific job, an **application record** is created. This is where the actual recruitment work happens — like a **Kanban board**:
- **Pending** → just came in, not yet reviewed
- **Reviewed** → you looked at the CV
- **Shortlisted** → you want to interview them
- **Rejected** → not suitable

You drag and drop, or click a button, to move them along.

**Why it matters:** No more "wait, did we shortlist Kumar for the Qatar job or the Dubai one?" — every application is tied to one specific job, so there's no confusion.

---

### 2.6 Interviews
A **calendar of every interview scheduled**, by date, time, and recruiter.
- Schedule a new interview directly from a candidate's profile
- Add notes after the interview (pass / fail / call again)
- See today's interviews on the home page

**Why it matters:** No double bookings, no forgotten interviews. The system also keeps a record of every interview decision — so 6 months later we can see *"Why did we reject Nimal?"* and the notes are right there.

---

### 2.7 Messages (Communications log)
This is your **black box recorder**. Every WhatsApp message in, every WhatsApp message out, every email — all in one timeline, per candidate.

**Why it matters:** If a candidate says *"Madam, you promised me 1500 dollars salary"* — you open his record and see exactly what was sent. No "he said / she said". Complete transparency.

---

### 2.8 CV Manager
A **library of every CV** the system has ever received.
- Original file (PDF, Word, photo of a paper CV)
- The system reads the CV automatically using AI and **extracts the information** — name, age, experience, skills, qualifications, languages — into clean fields
- You can search across all CVs by skill: *"show me everyone who has welding experience"*

**Why it matters:** A CV is no longer a file sitting in a folder. It becomes **searchable data**. You can find your next welder candidate without opening a single PDF.

---

### 2.9 Knowledge Base
This is the **brain of the chatbot**. Whatever you write here, the chatbot can answer.
- Company policies ("How long does processing take?")
- Salary ranges per country
- Visa requirements
- Frequently asked questions

You type the answer once. The chatbot answers it 1,000 times — in three languages.

**Why it matters:** Right now, your team answers the same questions all day: *"What's the salary?" "How long for visa?"* — that time goes back to you.

---

### 2.10 General Pool
A **reserve bench** of candidates who applied for jobs that are now closed, or who didn't quite fit at the time, but **we want to keep them**.

When a new matching job opens, you can pull them straight back in. No CV is ever lost.

---

### 2.11 Analytics
The **business intelligence corner**. Charts and numbers for management:
- Where are candidates coming from? (Ads, referrals, walk-ins)
- Which jobs fill fastest?
- How long does it take from "applied" to "hired"?
- Conversion rate at each stage

**Why it matters:** Decisions stop being gut-feel and start being data-driven. *"Sri Lankan drivers convert at 70%, Bangladeshi at 30% — let's focus the ads."*

---

### 2.12 Admin Dashboard (only for the boss)
The **control room** — only the administrator sees this.
- Create staff accounts
- Decide who sees what (a Project Handler doesn't need to see all CVs; a Sourcing Officer does)
- Reset passwords, lock accounts, audit who did what

**Roles in the system:**
| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Sees and controls everything |
| **Sourcing Department** | Manages candidates, CVs, applications — the heavy lifters |
| **Project Handler** | Sees the projects assigned to them, the candidates in those projects, and the messages |

## 3. THE COMPLETE USE-CASE SCENARIO — One Candidate, Beginning to End

**Now I want to show you how all these pieces work together. Let's follow ONE candidate — let's call him *Saman Perera*, a driver in Kandy, looking for a job in Saudi Arabia.**

> *(This is the part that ties the whole system together. Walk through it slowly.)*

---

### Step 1 — Saman sees a Facebook ad
Dewan runs a Facebook ad: *"Heavy vehicle drivers wanted for Saudi Arabia. Tap to apply on WhatsApp."*
Saman taps the ad. His WhatsApp opens with a pre-typed message that includes a hidden code telling the bot *"this person came from the Saudi driver ad."*

### Step 2 — The chatbot greets him in his language
The bot sees he typed in Sinhala. It replies in Sinhala:
> *"Ayubowan! Mama Dewan Recruitment ge AI assistant. Oyaa Saudi Arabia Heavy Vehicle Driver job ekata application karanawa kiyala dannawa. Mata oyaage CV eka ewanna puluwanda?"*

**Notice three things:**
1. It knew the **language** without asking
2. It knew the **job** because of the ad click
3. It skipped straight to **CV upload** — no boring 10-question form

### Step 3 — Saman uploads his CV (photo from his phone)
The bot accepts the photo. Behind the scenes, AI:
- Reads the photo (even if it's slightly blurry)
- Extracts his name, age, NIC, license type, years of driving experience, languages
- If something is missing (say, age) — the bot asks **only for that**, not the whole form again

### Step 4 — The bot answers his questions naturally
Saman asks in Singlish: *"Salary eka kohomada machan?"*
The bot checks the Knowledge Base, finds the salary range, replies in the same friendly Singlish style:
> *"Aiya, Saudi heavy driver job eke salary range eka SAR 2,500 to 3,500. Accommodation free, food allowance separate. Interested da?"*

### Step 5 — Application is complete — appears in YOUR dashboard automatically
The moment Saman uploads his CV, **the Sourcing team's dashboard pings**:
- A new candidate appears in the **Candidates** list
- A new application appears under the **Saudi Driver job**, in the "Pending" column
- His full conversation is logged under **Messages**
- His CV is in **CV Manager**, fully parsed

**Nobody typed a single thing. The chatbot did it all.**

### Step 6 — Sourcing officer reviews
Priya in Sourcing opens the dashboard at 9 AM. She sees:
- 14 new applications overnight
- Saman is one of them
- One click on his name → she sees his CV, his photo, his conversation, his experience
- She likes the profile → drags him to **"Shortlisted"**

### Step 7 — Interview scheduled
Priya clicks "Schedule Interview" on Saman's profile.
- Picks a date and time
- Adds her own name as the interviewer
- The interview appears on the **Interviews calendar**
- (Optionally, an automated WhatsApp goes to Saman: *"Your interview is on Wednesday at 10 AM. Please reply YES to confirm."*)

### Step 8 — Interview happens, notes are recorded
After the interview, Priya goes to the interview record:
- Marks **PASS**
- Adds notes: *"Strong Arabic basics, 8 years GCC experience, ready to travel immediately"*
- Moves the application to **"Selected"**

### Step 9 — Project Handler sees the win
The Project Handler running the Al-Rashid project opens the **Projects** page:
- Saudi Driver job — **1 more selected** ✓
- Vacancies remaining: 11 → 10

### Step 10 — If something goes wrong, you have full history
3 months later, if there's any dispute about what was promised — you open Saman's record. **Every message, every CV version, every interview note, every status change is there.** Forever.

## 7. SUGGESTED Q&A PROMPTS (likely questions from the room)

**Q: What if the chatbot makes a mistake?**
A: Every conversation is logged. You can step in any time and reply manually from the Messages tab.

**Q: What if a candidate doesn't have WhatsApp?**
A: You can still add them manually from the Candidates page — the system works for both.

**Q: What if the internet goes down?**
A: The bot is hosted on Google Cloud — even if our office internet drops, the bot keeps replying to candidates. When you reconnect, everything is waiting in the dashboard.

**Q: Will I have to learn coding?**
A: Zero coding. If you can use WhatsApp and Facebook, you can use this. Today's session itself is the training.

**Q: What about data security — candidate phone numbers, NICs?**
A: Everything is stored on secure Google Cloud servers, password-protected, role-restricted. Only authorised Dewan staff can see candidate data.

**Q: Can we change the questions the bot asks?**
A: Yes — through the **Knowledge Base**. You type, the bot learns. No developer needed.
